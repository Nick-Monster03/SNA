
==========================================================================================
H2: DISTANCE, LANGUAGE AND FLOWS (MRQAP, DOUBLE SEMI-PARTIALLING)
==========================================================================================
Sample: 276 regions with GDP (UK, IS, LI excluded), 71,370 ordered cross-country pairs (same-country pairs excluded: zero flow by construction).
p-values: 1000 DSP permutations of rows and columns (two-sided, on the t-value).
Dependent variable: weighted = log(1 + students/year); binary_b5 = 1 if >= 5 students/year (linear probability model).
  M1_roadmap        log_distance, same_language, same_family, gdp_difference
  M2_size           log_distance, same_language, same_family, gdp_difference, log_out_strength_origin, log_in_strength_destination
  M3_fixed_effects  log_distance, same_language, same_family, gdp_difference + one effect per origin and per destination
